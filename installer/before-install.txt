Voice Clone Studio

BEFORE YOU INSTALL

  - Keep the setup .exe and ALL of its .bin files in the same folder.
  - You need Windows 10 or 11 and an NVIDIA graphics card with 8 GB of memory
    (RTX 20, 30, 40 or 50 series).
  - Your NVIDIA driver must be version 572 or newer. Update it with the
    NVIDIA app or from nvidia.com/drivers if you are not sure.
  - About 30 GB of free disk space is needed during installation.
  - The first analysis and the first generation after installing take several
    minutes, because Windows checks every new file once. This is normal.

RESPONSIBLE USE

  This app creates a digital copy of a voice. Only create a voice profile from
  a recording of YOUR OWN voice, or of someone who has clearly agreed to it.
  The app asks the speaker to read a consent statement aloud before it will
  generate anything.

  Every file it generates is watermarked and labelled as AI-generated speech.
  Do not use it to impersonate, deceive, harass or defraud anyone. Doing so
  may be a criminal offence.

Everything runs on your computer. Your recordings are not uploaded anywhere.
